The Problem

One degree matters.

Nearly half of cataract patients also have astigmatism. Correcting it takes a toric lens placed within 2 to 3 degrees of a precise target axis.

Most surgeons still mark the eye by hand with ink. The rest buy dedicated digital alignment systems that most clinics cannot justify.
